## Browser extension (`extension/`)

The popup ran its own palette and motion, which design.md rules out. Fixed in `4c63ad1` and `3e63f96`:

- Tokens carry the app's Emerald theme values. The purple gradient Ramadan banner with blurred glow orbs is now a plain surface, with accent gold for emphasis.
- Three infinite pulse and glow animations removed; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
- 27 font sizes raised from 8-11px to 12px; section labels in written case.
- Focus ring added. The notification toggle had black text on the dark header (1.22:1). The footer link is underlined. Small icon buttons are 24px.
- Glyph buttons (`↻`, `−`, `+`, `EN`) have names in both languages. `<html lang>` follows the language toggle.
- **Result:** axe-core, WCAG 2.2 AA, found 0 violations in all 6 combinations of two languages and three views, at 380px, with no overflow and no text under 12px.

Publishing the new popup to the Chrome Web Store needs a version bump and upload; not done here.

## Click-through (R-35)

**Method:**
- Every visible enabled button, tab, switch and combobox on each guest-reachable route was clicked with a full pointer sequence in a 390px frame.
- The result was recorded as navigated, opened a dialog, or changed the page.
- Every dialog that opened was closed with Escape. Any dialog that seemed stuck was re-checked with a real key press before being counted.
- Every internal link was requested.
- Skipped as destructive or outward: delete, reset, log out, pay, donate, send, submit, sign in.
