Nawaetu Design Audit

Nawaetu does not read as AI-generated design. It has a written direction, one semantic token layer across five themes, an icon registry and honest copy. What it has instead is drift: feature code has outgrown its own design.md, and the gaps show up as contrast failures, unnamed controls and 6 to 11px text.

Update, same day: all 28 findings fixed, and the follow-up pass is done. That pass covered a click-through of every guest-reachable control, the browser extension, the Android wrapper and the Indonesian-only UI text.

Each fix is its own commit on a branch. The final re-scan found 0 axe violations on 22 routes and in 50 runs across 5 themes, no blur, no text under 12px and no overflow at 320px. See After the fixes.

Delivery Gate

antislop's four-block check, after the fixes. Each row shows what failed in the audit.

1. Hard Gate

PASS

Every rule passes for everything a guest can reach. R-35's click-through recorded every control on every route; dialogs that seemed stuck on Escape were re-checked with a real key press, and the click-through found five defects, all fixed. Signed-in pages were checked afterwards with a throwaway account; that found and fixed unnamed mentor and profile controls, a non-dialog history drawer, and settings stored with layers of JSON quotes. Before: FAIL on R-02 em dashes, R-03 tap targets, R-25 contrast, R-32 keyboard, R-34 dead theme styles, R-39 names and landmarks.

2. Purpose-Gate

PASS

Gradients, Sparkles, uppercase, blur and motion each have a written rule in design.md. Before: FAIL on R-01, R-04, R-06, R-10, R-19.

3. Liveliness

PASS

Dials are declared as ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. There is one focal point per screen, one accent, and an identity motif of Arabic script, emerald and warm paper. Before: FAIL, no dials declared.

4. Craftsmanship and Quality Locks

PASS

CTAs name their action, colors come from the token palette, and runtime CSS patches are gone. Before: FAIL on R-15, R-29, R-31.

Findings

Numbered for the fix request. Priority follows the rule tier. Findings marked Seen in browser were confirmed in the running app.

Showing all 28 findings.

  1. 01HighR-25

    Delete and danger buttons are unreadable in every dark theme

    The destructive Button puts white text on --color-danger, which is #F87171 in Emerald, Midnight and Sunset: 2.77:1 against a 4.5:1 minimum. The intended dark fallback never applies (see 08).

    • src/components/ui/button.tsx:32, src/context/themes.ts (danger)

    Fix: give dark themes a dangerForeground of near-black, or a deeper danger fill for buttons.

  2. 02HighR-25Seen in browser

    Primary-colored text fails on Midnight cards

    138 places use text-[rgb(var(--color-primary))]. Light themes remap it to the strong shade, dark themes do not. Midnight #3B82F6 on its card surface is 3.98:1; axe found the same class of failure at 3.59:1 on /quran.

    • src/app/globals.css:474 (light-only remap)

    Fix: point text use at --color-primary-strong in every theme and give Midnight a lighter strong value.

  3. 03HighR-25 · R-29

    Off-palette streak and stats colors ignore the theme

    Hardcoded sky-400, violet-400 and orange-400 measure 2.14, 2.72 and 2.26:1 on white, below even the 3:1 bar for icons.

    • src/components/streak/StreakWeek.tsx:44,69, src/components/StreakBadge.tsx:222
    • src/components/stats/StatsOverview.tsx:156, src/components/auth/LoginNudge.tsx:86

    Fix: map streak freeze to --color-info and the rest to existing tokens.

  4. 04HighR-25 non-text

    Input borders are close to invisible

    Form fields need a 3:1 boundary. Measured: 1.05 to 1.08:1 in the dark themes (the border token at 16% alpha), 1.33:1 in Daylight, 1.44:1 in Blossom.

    • src/app/globals.css:109-110, src/context/themes.ts (border)

    Fix: add an --color-border-strong for inputs and controls; keep the quiet border for card separation.

  5. 05HighR-03 · R-25 · design.mdSeen in browser

    Most text on small phones is under 12px, some of it 7px

    418 arbitrary sizes in code (text-[10px] 226, [9px] 103, [11px] 52, [8px] 22, [7px]/[6px] 15). At 320px wide the browser shows it plainly: 120 of 121 text elements on /hadith and 118 of 119 on /dua render below 12px, because globals.css also shrinks text-xs and text-sm on screens up to 390px.

    • 7px: dhikr counter label (DhikrDisplay.tsx:135), level badge on /stats, prayer badges on home
    • src/app/globals.css:336-343 (the shrink)

    Fix: delete the shrink, then replace arbitrary sizes with a 12px floor (text-xs) for metadata.

  6. 06HighR-02

    Em dashes in user-facing copy, including the app name

    About 40 strings in both locales, plus Nawaetu — Worship with Intention as the install name and every tab title. Licensed HadeethEnc and QuranEnc text is excluded.

    • src/app/_manifest.ts:28, src/components/DynamicTitle.tsx:33,37
    • translations/{en,id}/other.ts, ramadhan.ts, hijri-calendar.ts, fasting-tracker.ts, quran.ts, rewards.ts
    • Empty-value glyph in QuranStatsCard.tsx:43,51,59

    Fix: colon, comma or period. The dash before a hadith source is a quotation convention and is left for the owner to decide.

  7. 07HighR-39Seen in browser

    Buttons show an arrow cursor, not a hand

    Tailwind v4 resets buttons to cursor: default and nothing restores it. In the browser: 39 of 46 controls on a surah page, 30 of 36 on /missions, 13 of 24 on /settings.

    Fix: one base rule for enabled button, [role=button], summary, label[for].

  8. 08HighR-34

    Every dark: style in the primitives is dead

    @custom-variant dark waits for a .dark class that nothing sets; themes use data-color-mode. The destructive, outline and input dark recipes never apply.

    • src/app/globals.css:5

    Fix: @custom-variant dark (&:where([data-color-mode="dark"], [data-color-mode="dark"] *));

  9. 09HighR-34 · R-26

    Broken color values silently drop backgrounds

    • rgb(var(--color-secondary)) becomes rgb(rgb(...)): About page CTA and section, profile header. about/page.tsx:151,283, ProfileHeader.tsx:42
    • color-mix() on raw RGB channels: all 15 declarations in RamadhanHeroCard.tsx are invalid, so its gradient never paints.

    Fix: use rgb(var(--color-surface-subtle)) and rgb(var(--color-primary-light) / 0.2).

  10. 10HighR-03 · R-32

    36px inputs, and a Zen mode with no keyboard path

    Input is h-9 while design.md sets 44px; the global 44px rule skips inputs. Zen mode counts through a full-screen div with onClick (DhikrZenMode.tsx:74).

    Fix: h-11 on Input; add Space and Enter handling or a real button in Zen mode.

  11. 24HighR-39Seen in browser

    Over 160 controls have no accessible name

    A screen reader announces these as "button" or "link" with nothing else.

    • 115 quick-play buttons on /quran: icon only, 28px on desktop. SurahList.tsx:436
    • 37 verse actions on a surah page: the label lives only in a tooltip. VerseItem.tsx:120-200
    • Back links with only a chevron on /journal, /missions, /settings and surah pages. journal/page.tsx:147
    • 5 date tiles and an icon button on /settings; an aria-expanded on an element that cannot expand
    • Language buttons on /sirah/concise whose label does not contain their visible text

    Fix: aria-label from translations on each icon-only control; mark the icons aria-hidden.

  12. 25HighR-32Seen in browser

    Surah links remove the focus ring

    Every surah title link has outline-none with no replacement. Tabbing down the surah list shows no position at all: 38 of the first 80 focusable elements on /quran had no visible indicator.

    • src/components/quran/SurahList.tsx:399

    Fix: delete outline-none; the base :focus-visible ring already works.

  13. 26HighR-25Seen in browser

    The status badge recipe fails contrast

    Badges put a status color on its own 10% tint, often at reduced opacity. The tokens pass on plain surfaces, but not on the tint: success 4.38, warning 4.31, gold 4.35, danger at 80% 3.91, primary at 70% 3.62 in Daylight. Blossom drops to 2.27:1 on /missions.

    • axe, Daylight at 320px: 25 on /dua, 13 on /hadith, 11 on /missions. Blossom: 25, 13 and 5. Dark themes: 3 each on /missions.

    Fix: one shared badge component using the strong status shade at full opacity for text.

  14. 27HighR-32Seen in browser

    Horizontal tag strips cannot be scrolled by keyboard

    Hidden-scrollbar rows hold content past the edge with nothing focusable inside: 12 on /hadith, 9 on /dua, 1 on /stats.

    Fix: let the tags wrap; or give the strip tabindex="0" and a label.

  15. 28HighR-39Seen in browser

    Half the routes have no main landmark

    12 of 23 routes render without <main>, including /quran, /stats, /settings and /about. /qibla has no h1; /dhikr has two.

    Fix: one <main> in the root layout around page content.

  16. 11MediumR-10

    Backdrop blur in 65 files

    118 backdrop-blur utilities against 13 uses of the sanctioned glass-surface: cards, dialogs (backdrop-blur-3xl in DhikrHistory), sticky headers, the chat input. Many sit on an opaque surface, where blur paints nothing and only costs GPU on low-end Android.

    Fix: keep blur on the bottom nav only; delete the rest.

  17. 12MediumR-04

    Sparkles as a default icon

    96 references. Defensible for Lailatul Qadr; not for "early mission", About, insights or check-in success, and it spins as a loading indicator in StoryShareModal.tsx:262.

    Fix: write the reason per use, or swap for a content icon. Use the existing spinner for loading.

  18. 13MediumR-04 · design.md

    112 files import Lucide directly

    Lucide is a documented choice, so it passes. Bypassing AppIcon skips the registry's size, tone and aria-hidden handling, which is how a lightning icon reached the stats grid.

    Fix: an ESLint no-restricted-imports rule for lucide-react outside AppIcon.

  19. 14MediumR-01 · R-19

    A mesh gradient that animates forever

    RamadhanHeroCard stacks a linear gradient, three radial orbs, blur and a 4s infinite animate-gradient-shift. Reduced motion stops it. Today it is also invisible (see 09).

    Fix: give it one job (deepen across the three 10-day periods) and drop the loop.

  20. 15MediumR-01 · R-29

    Rainbow stat tiles

    Four tiles, four washes: orange, primary, yellow, violet. Five hues on one screen with no hierarchy reason. StatsOverview.tsx:136-160

    Fix: primary for the one stat that matters, neutral surfaces for the rest.

  21. 16MediumR-06 · AGENTS.md

    Tracked uppercase labels in 73 files

    170 uppercase tracking-wider lines. Not extreme spacing, but at this dose and at 9 to 10px it becomes a house look. Includes an untranslated, hardcoded HARI KE-{viewDay} — LOKASI SHOLAT WAJIB (DailyPrayerTracker.tsx:138).

    Fix: sentence case for labels; move the hardcoded string to both locales.

  22. 17LowR-33 spirit · R-31

    globals.css patches components by matching class strings

    Selectors such as [class*="from-[rgb(var(--color-primary))"] with !important restyle components at runtime per theme (globals.css:186-198, 474-523). Brittle to any class change, and why 02 is only half fixed.

  23. 18LowR-03

    Utilities silently redefined at 390px

    .px-3, .py-3, .gap-3, .text-xs, .text-sm change meaning on small screens (globals.css:320-344). Fix together with 05.

  24. 19Lowdesign.md

    Emoji in product copy

    Toast fallbacks (GuestSyncManager.tsx:188,373, PrayerCheckInWidget.tsx:237), push titles (push-copy.ts:19,51), a greeting (time-context.ts:109).

  25. 20LowR-15

    One generic CTA

    "Mulai Sekarang" (id/other.ts:681). Name the action, for example "Tulis niat hari ini".

  26. 21LowLiveliness

    No declared dials

    Suggested line for design.md, for the owner to confirm: a daily worship companion PWA for Indonesian Muslims, in a calm, warm emerald-and-paper language, ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

  27. 22Lowantislop-code

    Dead and duplicated CSS

    Two identical reduced-motion blocks, an empty rule, banner comments, and a header that still calls Daylight a "Light Glassmorphism Design System".

  28. 23LowR-39

    LocaleContext corrects it after hydration (the browser showed en), but server HTML always says id. layout.tsx:180

What passes

  • R-03No horizontal overflow on any of the 23 routes at 320px. Element boxes were measured, so the overflow-x: clip on body could not hide a leak.
  • R-17 R-18 R-36No user counts, ratings, testimonials, compliance or speed claims in either locale. The one statistic is a cited hadith.
  • R-16No buzzwords. "The ultimate goal" in journey.ts is literal.
  • R-24 R-26Debug routes return 404 in production and their links only render in development. No nav item points at a missing page.
  • R-21Light Daylight is the default; four more themes are a real choice.
  • R-37design.md exists and is specific.
  • R-12All 252 shadows use the two shadow tokens. No colored glow.
  • R-39 motionReduced motion and reduced transparency are both handled. Zoom is not blocked.
  • R-40No raw img; media uses next/image. Lottie loads after first paint.
  • Tap sizeOnly inline text links fall under 24px, which WCAG exempts.
  • ConsoleNo errors after load on any route, and the Next.js overlay reported no issues.

Method and limits

  • Rulebookantislop core (R-01 to R-40) and its UI, human, layoutmobile, copywriting and code skills, judged against Nawaetu's own design.md.
  • CodePattern sweeps over src/ without tests. Token contrast from anti-slop/theme-contrast.mts, failing pairs re-checked with antislop's contrast-check.py.
  • BrowserDev server, every route in a 320px frame: element overflow, tap sizes, cursor, keyboard focus ring on the first 80 focusable elements, text sizes, axe-core. Contrast re-run in the four other themes at 390px on the ten busiest routes.
  • Not doneSending a mentor message, sending the rewards form, and payment: they reach real services. axe skips text over gradients and blur, so its contrast counts are a floor. The click-through, the extension, the Android wrapper and signed-in pages are covered in fix reports 003 and later. A re-sweep on 25 November (fix report 004) closed leftovers of findings 6, 11, 12, 16 and 17.
