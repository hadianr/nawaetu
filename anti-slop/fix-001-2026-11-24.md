## Side effects worth knowing

- **Visual change:** small labels are larger and some tiles wrap. The home header location pill is icon-only under 389px so the user's name fits; its label stays for screen readers. The Ramadan hero is calmer, so finding 14 is resolved by the same edit, and the stat tiles lost their washes (finding 15).
- **Not fixed by design:** the quiet card border stays at 1.05 to 1.44:1. It separates cards and is not a control boundary. Accent as text still fails in Daylight and Blossom, and nothing uses it as text.
- **Locked missions** keep their dimmed look. WCAG exempts inactive controls, and axe flags them only when a locked mission is on screen.
- `DailyPrayerTracker` still has hardcoded Indonesian strings beyond the one fixed here.
